# Review: T-022-AP-research.md (AP-11 .. AP-21)

Reviewed against `docs/specs/T-022-movement-speed.md` (the "spec") and `evidence/INDEX.md`. Read-only review; no REA/Ghidra runs were made.
Checks: (1) invented game facts, (2) address/name/row citations, (3) concreteness of "Done when", (4) REA step realism and run budget (1-3 runs, ~4 min each; 0x7722f5 and 0x7e5264 have no Ghidra function), (5) snapshot/file reuse, (6) coverage.

Totals: 3 high, 7 medium, 14 low.
